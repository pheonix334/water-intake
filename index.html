How it works

  1. Enter your weight and activity level. More activity means more fluid loss, so the goal adjusts upward.
  2. Get your daily goal. The calculator uses a common formula — roughly 33ml per kg of bodyweight — then scales it by your activity level.
  3. Log glasses as you drink. Tap the + button each time you finish a glass (counted as 250ml). The progress bar fills up toward your goal.
  4. It resets daily. Your tracker automatically starts fresh each day, while your goal stays saved.

This is a general estimate — actual needs vary with climate, exercise intensity, health conditions, pregnancy, and medications.
